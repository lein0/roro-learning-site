Privacy Policy

Ro Ro Learning (bundle ID eml.TraaiinMe), published by Eric Leino
Effective date: November 11, 2026
Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version: Ro Ro does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you have iCloud turned on). We never receive your voice. Children can use Ro Ro safely.

Who we are

Ro Ro Learning (“Ro Ro”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS and iPadOS language-learning app published by Eric Leino. You can reach us at eric.leino@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate a server that receives data from the app. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage data.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps these things on your device:

  • The language you are learning and the choices you make while setting up (for example a first name, a country and a city, if you choose to enter them, used only to personalise phrases on your screen)
  • Your learning progress: phrases learned, streaks, points, stars, achievements and Journey stages
  • App settings such as theme, speech speed and voice choice

This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app removes it from your device. You can also reset progress inside the app.

iCloud sync (optional)

If iCloud is turned on for Ro Ro on your Apple ID, your progress and settings are stored in your own iCloud Key-Value storage so they can follow you across your devices. That storage belongs to you, is provided and protected by Apple, and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in iOS Settings.

Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

The Speak game lets you say a phrase out loud and checks your pronunciation. It only works if you grant permission, and only after the app explains why. You can use every other part of Ro Ro without it, and you can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings.

  • The microphone is only on while you are playing a Speak question.
  • Your speech is turned into text using Apple’s Speech framework. Recognition happens on your device whenever your device supports it for that language.
  • If your device cannot do it on-device, iOS may send the audio to Apple’s speech recognition service, which is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Ro Ro does not record, save, keep or receive your voice, and no audio ever reaches us.

Hearing phrases read aloud

Phrases are read aloud with the voices built into your device, which are generated on your device. You can also download optional “Natural voices”. See the next section.

Optional voice downloads

If you choose to download Natural voices (Profile → Speech Settings), the app downloads open-source voice model files from huggingface.co. This is an ordinary file download. The app sends no personal information or learning data. As with any download from the internet, the host can see your device’s IP address and the file you asked for, under its own privacy policy. Once downloaded, the voices run entirely on your device and work offline. If you never download a voice pack, the app makes no such request.

City suggestions during setup (optional)

When you type a city during setup, the app may use Apple’s MapKit search to suggest matching places. The text you type is sent to Apple to produce suggestions, under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive it, and the app does not track your location. You can skip this step.

Children’s privacy

Ro Ro is rated 4+ and is suitable for learners of all ages, including children. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (and children under 16 in the EU/UK). The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no chat or social features, no in-app purchases, and no links that take a child to other websites except the privacy, terms and support pages (and a mail link to us) that adults may open from the About screen.

This is meant to be consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR rules for children. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us at eric.leino@gmail.com with any question. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, tell us and we will delete it.

Support emails and this website

If you email us, we receive the email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we keep it only as long as needed. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not use cookies, analytics or trackers on this website.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not have any.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • We do not build profiles of you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app to remove all local data from the device.
  • Reset progress in the app, or turn off iCloud for Ro Ro in iOS Settings.
  • Turn the microphone and speech recognition permissions on or off in iOS Settings.
  • Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you emailed us and want that message deleted, write to us and we will do it. Residents of California, the EU, the UK and elsewhere who want to exercise a privacy right can contact us at the address below.

Third parties

Ro Ro relies on Apple services built into iOS: text-to-speech, speech recognition, iCloud Key-Value storage, MapKit search and App Store features. Those are covered by Apple’s privacy policy. Optional voice downloads come from huggingface.co. We do not use any other third-party service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change would affect how personal information is handled, we will make it clear in the app’s release notes. Because the app collects no personal information today, we expect changes to be rare.
