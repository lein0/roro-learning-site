Ro Ro Learning

Learn a language in minutes. For iPhone and iPad.

Ro Ro teaches everyday phrases in Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, Spanish (Mexican), French, German and Hindi through short, friendly games, a story-driven Journey, speaking practice and spaced review. There is no account to make, no ads, and nothing is tracked. It works offline.
