Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. Ro Ro works offline. You only need internet to download the optional Natural voices.

How do I get more natural voices?

Open Profile → Speech Settings, choose Natural voices, and download a voice for your language. After that it works offline. You can delete it again at any time to free up space.

The Speak game can’t hear me.

Check that Ro Ro has Microphone and Speech Recognition permission in iOS Settings → Ro Ro Learning. If you prefer not to speak, every other game works without them.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

If iCloud is on for Ro Ro, your progress syncs through your own iCloud. Otherwise it stays on the device it was made on.

Is Ro Ro safe for children?

Yes. It is rated 4+, has no ads, accounts, chat or tracking, and collects no personal information. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Reset progress in the app, or delete the app. Anything in your iCloud can be managed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.
